Frequently asked questions

Before you get started.

Clear expectations help us deliver the right result for your property marketing.

What documents can I provide?

You can submit existing floor plans, architectural drawings, available cadastral information or other relevant property documents. We will assess whether the information is suitable for the requested output.

Can you work from cadastral information alone?

Sometimes it may help identify or contextualise a property, but it may not contain the internal layout or verified dimensions. We confirm feasibility after reviewing the available source information.

What file formats will I receive?

The standard agreed delivery formats are PDF and PNG/JPG. Any additional formats or editable source files must be agreed in writing.

Do you offer fixed prices?

Pricing is available on request. Each quotation depends on the source material, property type, complexity, number of plans, requested presentation and agreed delivery schedule.

Is this an official or certified architectural drawing?

No, not by default. The service is intended for consumer understanding and property marketing. Official, cadastral, permitting or construction use requires separate confirmation and may require a qualified local professional.

Do B2B terms and contracts apply?

Yes. Services are provided to business clients under the applicable written quotation, service agreement and terms and conditions. Work begins after the scope and commercial terms have been accepted.

How do you handle confidential property documents?

Document submission and processing should follow the agreed confidentiality and data-handling arrangements. Please do not send sensitive documents until an approved submission method and applicable terms are in place.
